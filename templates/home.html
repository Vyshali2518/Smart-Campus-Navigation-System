How to use CampusNav

You can move between pages with the navigation bar at the top. Start here for a quick tour.

01 · Find a place

Explore the Map

Open Map to see campus locations. Use the search box or category filter to find a place, then select Show to focus the map or open its marker for more details.

02 · Plan a trip

Choose your route

On the Map page, choose a starting location, a destination, and walking, cycling, or car. Select Find real routes to request routes based on OpenStreetMap data.

03 · Compare results

Read the route details

Each result shows its mapped line, distance, and estimated travel time. Select a result to highlight it on the map, and expand Turn-by-turn directions for its instructions. Alternatives appear only when the routing service provides them.

04 · Check campus updates

Browse Events

Open Events to search or filter listings. You can add, edit, or delete demonstration events. Their status updates automatically to Over after the end date and time.

Accessibility and route data

Routes are supplied by OpenStreetMap via Valhalla and depend on location coordinates and available map data. They are not audited for step-free access or guaranteed to reflect current conditions.

Read the accessibility information

Before you travel

Location and event entries are demonstration data. Routes depend on OpenStreetMap coverage and do not guarantee accessibility or current conditions. Confirm important information with an official campus source.
