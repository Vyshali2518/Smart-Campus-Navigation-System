Indoor Positioning

Wi-Fi RSSI & BLE beacon registry. Used by the mobile companion app to estimate room-level position once GPS becomes unreliable indoors.

What is a beacon?

A small, battery-powered Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) sticker installed on a wall. It broadcasts a unique Beacon ID 10 times per second. When your phone walks past, it picks up that ID — the app then knows which room you're near.

Each row below is one physical beacon installed somewhere on campus.

What does RSSI mean?

RSSI = Received Signal Strength Indicator, measured in dBm (always negative for radio signals). It tells you how strong the beacon’s signal is when the phone hears it:

  • -40 dBm — right next to the beacon (~1 m)
  • -60 dBm — a few rooms away (~5–10 m)
  • -80 dBm — far / wall in between

The Reference RSSI column is what the signal should read at the labelled landmark. The phone compares live readings against the references to pinpoint the user (fingerprint localisation, accuracy ~3–5 m).
